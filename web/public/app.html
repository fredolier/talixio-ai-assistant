    Routines

    What the assistant does on a schedule: reminders, briefings, recurring reports. (Things to do are on the Tasks board.) Ask in chat to create them: remind me tomorrow at 9 to call the bank, every weekday at 8:00 send me a briefing.

    Morning briefing

    Mail to handle

    People

    From your emails, and what the assistant learns about each person.

    Background jobs

    Sub-agents the assistant starts for research and multi-step work. Each one has its own model tier and a cost cap; results come back in the chat.

    Memory

    Search everything you've discussed. Below, the files the assistant reads before every answer and updates as it learns; you can edit them too.

    Character and values

    Learned by itself from your history together: what matters to you, how you decide, and what kind of assistant it has become. A belief grows stronger when it is confirmed over time, weaker when contradicted, and fades when nothing confirms it. It weighs the established ones in its choices. "Forget" removes a belief for good.

    Skills

    Procedures it has learned for you. It reads the matching one before doing a task, and updates them as it learns. You can edit or delete them.

    Conversation digest

    The assistant keeps the latest messages word for word. Older ones are summarised, then each past week and month is condensed further: details fade, what matters stays. It reads this digest before every answer; everything remains searchable above.

      Google Calendar
      1. Open console.cloud.google.com, create a project (e.g. "Talixio").
      2. Enable the Google Calendar API.
      3. OAuth consent screen: type External, your email as contact. Under Audience, press Publish app ("In production"): in "Testing" Google cuts the access after 7 days. Google will show "unverified app" when you connect: that's your own app, continue.
      4. Create an OAuth client: type Web application, authorised redirect URI:
      5. Paste its client ID and secret in Configuration → Calendar below, save, then press Connect.
      Spotify

      Ask "play Daft Punk", "pause", "next song", "add this to my running playlist": the music plays on your phone or speaker. Needs Spotify Premium, and Spotify open (or used recently) on the device.

      1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard (sign in with your Spotify account) and press Create app: any name (e.g. "Talixio"), tick Web API, redirect URI:
      2. In the app's Settings, copy the Client ID and press View client secret.
      3. Paste both in Configuration → Spotify below, save, then press Connect.
      Connectors (MCP)

      Give the assistant tools from MCP servers: GitHub, Notion, your home automation, your own scripts… Local servers are started by command (Node servers need install.sh --with-node); remote ones by URL with a token in headers, or Sign in for services that use OAuth (Notion, Linear, Atlassian…). Tools that change something wait for your approval, unless the server has "trust": "all". Saved in data/mcp.json.
