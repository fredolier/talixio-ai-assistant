# Getting started

## Where do I talk to the assistant?
- **The web app** (computer and phone), at your server's address. Install it as an app:
  Chrome/Edge, the install icon in the address bar; Android, menu ⋮ → *Install app*;
  iPhone, Share → *Add to Home Screen*. You need an HTTPS address.
- **Telegram, Signal, email** and the terminal (`sudo talixio chat`): it is the same conversation everywhere.

## What it can do
- Answer live, search the web, read web pages.
- Remember you (editable memory on the Memory page) and find what was said.
- File and find your documents (Files), citing the page.
- Manage your tasks, reminders and routines (morning briefing…).
- Transcribe and summarise your meetings and voice notes.
- Read your email and calendar, and draft replies; anything that acts outside
  (sending an email, adding an event…) waits for your **approval**.

## Useful shortcuts
- Start a message with `/deep` for deeper reasoning, `/fast` for a quick answer.
- Under each answer: the model, the tokens, the estimated cost, and where your data went.
- **Stop** an answer in progress: the send button turns into *Stop the answer*.
- **Write the next message without waiting.** While an answer is on its way, type and send as usual (Enter, or the send
  button, which shows *Stop the answer* only while the box is empty): the message waits in line above the box and goes out by itself
  when the answer ends, one at a time and in order. A recording finished meanwhile waits the same way. ✎ takes a waiting
  message back into the box, ✕ cancels it. The line is kept in this browser if you close the page, per space. If a waiting
  message cannot be sent, the line stops there and offers *Send now*. Stopping an answer does not cancel the line.
- **Edit** your last message or ask for **another answer**.

## Find in the conversation
The 🔍 button at the top of the chat (*Find in the conversation*) searches the messages of the current space. Type at
least two letters: the messages with your words come first, then, after a few seconds, those close in meaning. Click a
result to jump to that message; *Close* (or Esc) hides the search.

## The message box
Enter sends; Shift+Enter starts a new line. The box grows with what you write, up to 40% of the window (at least about
ten lines), so you can see most of a long message; past that, it scrolls.

## Dictate
The 🎙️ button next to the message box (*Dictate*) types what you say: press it, speak, press it again. What you said is
transcribed and added to the box, after what is already there, so you can read it over before sending; the recording is
not kept. (The microphone button next to it is different: it records a voice note or a meeting.) The same 🎙️ is on
other text boxes too, such as the autonomy priorities in Pilotage. It needs a recent browser over HTTPS, access to the
microphone, and transcription turned on (see *Audio and meeting recordings*).

## The menu
On a computer, the menu on the left has eight entries: **Chat**, **Tasks**, **Files** (the library), **Apps**,
**Notifications**, **Mail**, **People** and, last, **Pilotage** (see *Pilotage*).
- **⋯ More** holds **Automations** (its **Routines** and **Missions** tabs: scheduled tasks and background work) and **Activity and costs**.
- Your name at the bottom opens the **account menu**: Settings, Usage (Activity and costs), Privacy, Memory, Language,
  Theme, *Install app* when your browser offers it, *Get help* (this FAQ) and Sign out.

On a phone, the bottom bar shows **Chat**, **Tasks**, **Files**, **Notifications** and **Pilotage**. **⋯ More** holds the
rest: Automations, Activity and costs, **Apps**, **Mail to handle**, **People**, **Memory**, **Privacy**, **Settings**,
*Install app* (when offered) and **Sign out**. There is no account menu on a phone: the theme is also in
**Settings → Essentials → Appearance**; Language and *Get help* are only in the account menu on a computer.

## Mail to handle
**Mail** (*Mail to handle*) sorts your new emails into **To answer**, **To do**, **To read** and **Low priority**. It needs
your mailbox connected (Settings → Connected accounts → *Your mailbox*) with *Sort new emails* turned on. **Check now**
sorts at once; it then checks every 15 minutes. Each email shows its sender, subject and a short summary, and a due date
when it has one.
- Move an email to another group with its selector: it remembers, and sorts similar emails the same way next time.
- **Prepare a reply** (in *To answer*) asks the assistant in the chat to draft one and save it as a draft.
- **→ Task** opens a new task filled in from the email, linked to it.
- **Done** puts it away; tick *show handled* to see those again, and **Reopen** one.

## People
**People** lists the people you get emails from, and what the assistant learns about each one. Search by name, company
or notes, or add someone with **New contact**. Click a person to edit their name, email, phone, company and *What you
know*, then **Save**. The card also shows their recent emails, where they appear in your documents and in your
conversations. **Ask about this person** starts a question in the chat; **Delete** asks you to confirm.

## Is the interface in the wrong language?
Account menu (on a computer) → **Language** (Automatic / Français / English): the choice is remembered in this browser.
The assistant itself answers in the language you write to it in. The **Theme** entry cycles System → Light → Dark.
